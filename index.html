Two steels,
one bar.

Hard carbon steel for the edge, bright nickel steel for the pattern, stacked and welded together in the fire at 1,150 °C.

Every fold
doubles it.

Cut, stack, hammer flat, back into the fire. Seven times. Two layers become four, then eight, then 256.

256 layers.
Now it cools.

Out of the fire the glow drains away, and an acid etch brings the layers up: dark carbon, bright nickel, a pattern no one can repeat.

Then we temper it

The colour
is the hardness.

Heat polished steel gently and it changes colour: straw, gold, bronze, blue. The lower the colour, the harder the edge. We temper each knife to one colour, polish the blade back to steel, and leave the colour on the spine.

The edge

Fifteen
degrees.

Each side is ground to fifteen degrees, then stropped on leather until the very edge is a thousand times thinner than a hair. Scroll in.
